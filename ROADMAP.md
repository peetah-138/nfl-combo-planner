are fine.)

## 2. Waiting on data (decisions with a date)

### The combo cut-off, from the combo lab (≈ 2–3 weeks)
- The lab paper-buys combos at 80–85, 85–90, 90–95, 95–100 and 100+% of fair, and replays smart sell on them.
- **Then:** if lower bands come out ahead *after* smart-sell exits (Pete's theory: cheap combos are fine if we rarely
  ride to the end), let the cut-off's self-adjustment use the lab's results, and revisit the 85% floor with Pete.
  Results › What's working › Combo price levels shows it as it fills in.

### The "Ready to promote?" bars (#541)
- The pass/fail bars on the Results card are first guesses (40 games, 15 latest games, 3 looks). **Waits on** real settled data passing through the card; **then** revisit the bars.

### Capped profit line for favorites (#375)
- Smart sell never takes a profit on a single bought above about 67c (its line is above what the bet can sell for). `exit_cap_shadow` (default off) records what a line capped 3c under the top would have done. **Waits on** that paper record and Pete's opt-in; **then** a live switch with old-versus-new logging.

### Self-tuning smart sell, first moves (≈ 1–2 weeks)
- Nothing to build: each league starts moving once it has ~30 settled bets with recorded game prices. Worth a check
  that the first changes (Results › Selling, "🎛️ What self-tuning changed") look sensible, and that the reasons read
  clearly. Since 2026.11.21.133 the exit numbers tune live behind `knobs_live` (#228 PR2-PR4); at release every league
  was too thin for a step, so nothing changed on day one.
- **#228 break_ok live:** turn `BRK_LIVE` on and add `brk_on` to `KNOB_LIVE` once a league has 40+ recorded break
  episodes that clear the bar (waits on data).
- **#228 PR7 rest:** judges for Smart Buy's simulator constants (`sb_win_min`, `sb_gain`, `sb_route_exit`, spreads) by
  re-simulating checked bets; `sb_ev_min` stays watch-only until split from `line_recommend`.
- **#228 hedge knobs live:** judge `hedge_lock_share` / `hedge_lock_cost` / the cap raise on how the games ended
  (`sale_outcomes`), with fresh-book costs, then add them to `KNOB_LIVE` and set `HEDGE_RAISE_LIVE` (the confirmation
  counts need a judge too). Follow-ups from PR4's reviews: #298-#302.
- **Learned selling guards, follow-ups:** make the flex-sell 85% backstop and smart sell more's `MORE_PROG_MIN` learned too,
  once #479 and #480 have records to learn from.

### Panel pipeline, long shots and in-play (issue #341, #348)
- **Long shots:** confirm Kalshi's fee on combo (RFQ) trades and set `LONGSHOT["fee_rate"]`; tune the leg band
  (65-80%) and the 7% hit floor from `longshot_log`, and learn a long shot's route (hold vs cash out) from its record
  once enough have settled.
- **In-play comeback model (#348, shipped, paper only for loosening):** after ~2 weeks of `comeback_shadow`, compare old vs
  new on settled checks per league and decide (data-gated) whether the new model may loosen; consider surfacing the other
  side only if its paper record clears the gate with positive dollars.
- **Combo sanity cap (#358/#361):** after ~2 weeks of the quotes table, look at how often quotes land over 120-130% of fair
  and how they settled, then tune `COMBO_SUSPECT` (data-gated); consider using the calibrated fair for the shown expected
  profit.
- **Market-anchored fair (#341 B):** once a league/type has 200+ settled markets in `mkt_fair`, add a held-out check,
  then decide with Pete whether the panel prices on it (the skip side first, stricter only).
- **Cost-carry combos (#341 C):** after a few weeks of paper results, decide whether near-even 2-3 leg combos become
  a panel kind.
- **Cover test lean threshold (#340):** tune `COVER["lean_fav"]` (75%) once weeks of results exist.
- **Prop price poller:** a public, key-free read of the prop series every 30 minutes, with a fair from the ESPN/DraftKings
  line, to give `prop_shadow` data (needs Pete's OK for the extra Kalshi requests).

### Calibration and the paper gate
- Fair-price calibration and the paper panel's record fill in on their own; just watch for oddities.

### Smarter spreads and totals (issue #117)
- **Part A shipped** (the margin-shape model, per-league gate, old vs new logged side by side). Its own record takes
  over the gate once a league has 200+ settled games of spread/total markets; watch Results › What's working.
- **Part B shipped** (CLV per strike, fillable size after fees, learned minimum edge per league; Build shows
  recommendations only; see `CLAUDE.md`). Watch Results › What's working › Bet types › "your bar and the close" as
  the first bars move (a league needs 30+ settled games at its bar).
- **Left as they are on purpose (selection changes, need Pete's OK):** NHL spreads are never priced today (ESPN's
  "details" for hockey is a moneyline, read as a 185-point spread, so the distance filter drops every puck line), and
  soccer spreads/totals aren't fetched at all (`_espn_board` gets a list of ESPN paths for soccer). The new model can
  price both (the backtest has them), but turning them on adds bets to the panel.
