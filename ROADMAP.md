## 2. Waiting on data (decisions with a date)

### The combo cut-off, from the combo lab (≈ 2–3 weeks)
- The lab paper-buys combos at 80–85, 85–90, 90–95, 95–100 and 100+% of fair, and replays smart sell on them.
- **Then:** if lower bands come out ahead *after* smart-sell exits (Pete's theory: cheap combos are fine if we rarely
  ride to the end), let the cut-off's self-adjustment use the lab's results, and revisit the 85% floor with Pete.
  Results › What's working › Combo price levels shows it as it fills in.

### Self-tuning smart sell, first moves (≈ 1–2 weeks)
- Nothing to build: each league starts moving once it has ~30 settled bets with recorded game prices. Worth a check
  that the first changes (Results › Selling, "🎛️ What self-tuning changed") look sensible, and that the reasons read
  clearly. Since 2026.11.21.133 the exit numbers tune live behind `knobs_live` (#228 PR2-PR4); at release every league
  was too thin for a step, so nothing changed on day one.
- **#228 PR5-PR7 (next):** loss guards / `break_ok` in shadow first (PR5), hedge knobs and the `hedge_cap` raise (PR6),
  Smart Buy simulator constants (PR7); split any shared knob first. Follow-ups from PR4's reviews: #298-#302.

### Calibration and the paper gate
- Fair-price calibration and the paper panel's record fill in on their own; just watch for oddities.

### Smarter spreads and totals (issue #117)
- **Part A shipped** (the margin-shape model, per-league gate, old vs new logged side by side). Its own record takes
  over the gate once a league has 200+ settled games of spread/total markets; watch Results › What's working.
- **Part B shipped** (CLV per strike, fillable size after fees, learned minimum edge per league; Build shows
  recommendations only; see `CLAUDE.md`). Watch Results › What's working › Bet types › "your bar and the close" as
  the first bars move (a league needs 30+ settled games at its bar).
- **Left as they are on purpose (selection changes, need Pete's OK):** NHL spreads are never priced today (ESPN's
  "details" for hockey is a moneyline, read as a 185-point spread, so the distance filter drops every puck line), and
  soccer spreads/totals aren't fetched at all (`_espn_board` gets a list of ESPN paths for soccer). The new model can
  price both (the backtest has them), but turning them on adds bets to the panel.

## 3. Ready to build (suggested order)

### a. Finish the makeover (Pete: "make it exceptional", like Kalshi's app; issue #12)
Phases 1, 2 and 3 shipped (phase 1: haptics and press feedback on everything, Live polish, push transition, hero
chart scrub; phase 2: live Games cards, score pulse, scrubbable game chart, the Sold moment, the big hit, Results
profit bars, skeletons; phase 3: every remaining screen (Build, Settings, Results, login, sheets) in the same motion
system, the reconnecting banner, error cards, pull to refresh everywhere, header collapse, safe areas, keyboard-aware
sheets, Let it ride detents, light mode, the recap streak note, panel sparklines; see `CLAUDE.md`). Kalshi/Novig
references and a frame-by-frame motion brief are in issue #12's comments. Still to do:
1. **Game page drill-down** (Kalshi live game page): team rows with scores, a live chance chart per side (or both on
   one chart; scrub is in), range switcher (LIVE/1D/1W), outlined pills with payout multipliers, a strip of other live
   games to hop between, a one-line plain-English summary. Coordinate with the faster-live work on the game page's refresh.
   Football (#72) has its field strip, play-by-play and the box score / team stats / injuries / last 5 / season leaders tabs;
   still to do: the same tabs for basketball, hockey, soccer, tennis and UFC, the trade ticker and pinned buy buttons.
2. **A shareable result card** (copy image / share) for Sold and Won, like Kalshi's "Position closed" card.
3. **Charts to drill into:** bet value over time with scrub (combo page), charts for Results › What's working.
   Panel cards' price line is from what the phone has seen; a server-side price history per panel bet would make it
   complete (needs an API field, so it was left out of the UI-only phase).
- Haptics (#60): method N (a see-through switch over every control) needs Pete's confirmation on his iPhone that
  every tap ticks. On iOS 27 nothing without a tap can tick (chart scrub, pull-to-refresh, sheet drag, tab swipe, the
  Let it ride slider's steps, a pattern's second tick). The safe-area / keyboard handling wants a look on the real
  Home Screen app.
- Rule: send Pete screenshots or a short clip before shipping visual changes.

### b. Smart Buy, stages 2 to 5 (issue #137; stage 1, the foundation, shipped: see CLAUDE.md)
1. **Calibrate the simulator** on the price recorder's paths (`price_path`) and `exit_calibration`: the pre-game
   drift (`SB_PRE_VOL`), how much of the gap to fair closes before the start (`SB_PRE_CLOSE`), in-play gap widening
   (`SB_LIVE_WIDEN`) and jump sizes, per league with borrowing; replay combos too (today only singles/spreads/totals).
2. **The gate: first slice shipped** (skip-only, learning dial per league, on by default: see CLAUDE.md). Still to
   do: Build "Best bets" shipped as a first slice (#63: one feed, singles/spreads/totals/value combos, recommend-only);
   still to do there: live games under way and sell-early favorites in the feed, a confidence line per league and type,
   realized return per type ("spreads: +6% over 40 bets"), a tappable detail page, the same gate on the Buy straight list
   and the daily panel's single pool (#137 comments); also scoring results under the policy (not only held to the end), and the graduation stages Pete opted
   into (own suggestions, then bigger stakes and extra auto-buys inside every cap with a losing-streak brake; Hard
   rule 2 in CLAUDE.md), which the Results card's "Bigger stakes: not yet" line tracks.
3. **A purchase carries a plan** (exit chance, target bid band, window, policy version), read by smart sell.
4. **Scoreboard and plan audit** (#138, #115): the scoreboard and the smart-sell scorecard shipped (see CLAUDE.md,
   Results page). Still to do: the weekly summary push, warnings/automatic tightening when a league or type loses
   beyond luck (learned minimum edge/exit chance, per league with borrowing, never loosened without data), links
   from each breakdown into its setting, tagging Live Buy idea buys apart from Build, and feeding the scorecard to
   `smart_tune` as its target (a selling change: needs Pete's go-ahead).
5. **Joint tuning** of both halves; continuation-value exits and partial exits (Pete's "dollars, not pennies").
   The weekly tuner's replay runs exit_policy (#228 PR0), the knob registry (PR1), the stepper and brake (PR2-PR4, live
   behind `knobs_live`) shipped; the self-tuning judge replays against real Kalshi offers (`_replay_real`, PR4). Still
   different from live in the plain `_replay`: take profit isn't replayed; the checkpoint x1.3 risk weight isn't
   modelled (recorded paths don't say which picks had won); the live
   risk weight's `swing_lambda` time-left shrink and Let it ride scaling aren't in the replay (it keeps `0.35 x swing`).
- **Smart Buy faster evidence, next** (#137, after #297): universe markets for the leagues the Mac's panel doesn't look
  at (soccer, tennis, EuroLeague, UFC: priced only by the web app's Build board today); game-winner backfill once a
  start time and result are recorded with each check; price paths for universe markets if exit calibration needs them
  (would grow `price_path`); the paper book choosing like the panel (choose_panel's ahead floor and type shares, fill
  caps).
- Finding from stage 1 to put to Pete: at the 1.5x profit floor, a single bought above ~67c can never be sold for a
  profit by smart sell (only held), and on modest-edge bets selling at the bid gives up part of the edge (the
  simulator's blended EV is below hold EV). Stage 5's continuation-value rule is meant to fix both.
