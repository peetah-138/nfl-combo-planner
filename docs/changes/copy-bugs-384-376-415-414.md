# Wording and small bugs: back-out notes, one sell-line phrase, long exit waits, just-bought markets (#384 #376 #415 #414)

**Section:** "Selling" (notes), "Live page" (sell line), "Build page" (Best bets)

- **#384** `combo_fail_note` (auto cash-out push when buyers back out) now ends "Still holding, and we'll keep watching:
  it tries again when the game or the price changes". The generic fallback no longer repeats the raw error text from
  `_accept_with_retries`; it says Kalshi didn't take the sale this time. Words only: when it sells, retries and the
  rate limit are unchanged.
- **#376** One phrase for an unreachable sell line, "no offer can beat waiting", on the do-now card, the combo row,
  `plan_words` and both Smart sell chips. The combo chip (`ssChip`) now shows the same hint singles had ("sells at $X+"
  or the phrase), reading the combo's `threshold`/`thr_over`.
- **#415** Best bets: `exitWhen(mins)` (`build_sections.js`) words the likely cash-out time from now: "about 41 min in"
  up to 90 min, then "in about 5 h" up to 2 days, then "in about 3 days". Not changed: the 73% itself (the simulator's
  `p_exit`, which includes pre-game drift, ranks the card); see the PR for the open question.
- **#414** `held_markets()` (single markets held on the account, bought in the last 10 minutes via `RECENT_BUYS`, or
  carrying an unclear-order block) is read by `build_page` to drop, from the page only, a held market (either side)
  from Best bets, the Single games card and the spreads/totals list. `buy_single` only records the ticker in
  `RECENT_BUYS` (a dict write); nothing about whether or how it buys changed, and the panel / auto paths do not read the
  list. Tests: `t_bestbets.py` (held_markets), `t_combobackout.py` (notes), `test_bestbets.py` (`exitWhen`),
  `test_singlesell_ui.py` (phrase on the chip and do-now card).
