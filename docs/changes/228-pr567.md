# Self-tuning PR5-PR7: the stop-loss as a 3-way choice, the loss guards and hedge numbers live, break_ok and Smart Buy watch-only (issue #228, #306)

**Section:** Selling (the stop-loss and loss-cut guards, auto-hedge guards, self-tuning), and Settings › Cash out & hedging.

**#306 (Pete: tighten).** A league's own record may no longer loosen four exit numbers below their start: `cut_accept`
(min 0.02), `exit_q_min` (min 0.5), `exit_est_smart` (min 0.95), `exit_est_tp` (min 0.9) now have `min` = `start` in the
registry, so `knob_candidates` only steps them stricter and `_knob_valid` ignores a value recorded below the start
before this change (the start is used).

**Selling at a loss: three choices (PR5).** Settings › Cash out & hedging › **Selling at a loss** replaces the old
on/off "Manual override" switch: **Never sell at a loss** (no automatic sale below what you paid: neither the stop-loss
nor the learned loss cut, combos and singles), **Let it tune itself** (the default) and **Allow (guarded)** (the old
switch on; every guard still holds). Setting `stop_loss_mode` (shared, synced to the watcher, `SL_MODES`); `sl_mode()`
reads it, and an older saved file with only `stop_loss` reads as Allow when it was on, else Let it tune itself (nothing
is rewritten; anything unreadable = Let it tune itself). The web app writes both, keeping `stop_loss` = (mode is Allow)
for older copies (the Pythonista app, a watcher not yet updated). Let it tune itself starts OFF in every league:
`sl_allowed(tickers)` is true only with self-tuning on and the league's **own** record having turned the new knob
`sl_auto` on (borrow `own`: never borrowed; off while its live brake holds). `sl_auto` is judged by the loss guards'
replay (`_sl_replay`: on = what selling at a loss would have got, on a sale price cut to 97% for this loosening step
(#285); off = held) on Pete's bar. Turning `sl_auto` on needs 3 standard errors (`KNOB_EVIDENCE_STRICT`), not 2. With Never, a single never sells below what it cost either. A tuned stop-loss sale says "Stop-loss (tuned for this league)" and logs `sl_mode`.
Pete's Mac today has `stop_loss` on, so it reads as Allow (guarded): unchanged.

**The loss guards and confirmation counts go live (PR5).** `sl_prog`, `sl_drop`, `sl_auto`, `sl_checks`, `sl_span`,
`sl_gap` are in `KNOB_LIVE`: with "Let smart sell tune its own numbers" on, `stoploss_params` and the new
`sl_confirm_params` read them through `knob_live_value` (league's own, else sport's, else everyone's, borrowed only
stricter, the start while braked; smart_tune's own `sl_*` record counts as the league's own, as before), and
smart_tune's `_sl_tune` only counts while the switch is on (one mover, #267). The confirmation counts are **split from
the hedge's** (`hedge_checks`, `hedge_span`, `hedge_cgap`, constants `HEDGE_CHECKS/SPAN/CGAP`, `hedge_confirm_params`);
`_sl_confirm`, `_sl_held`, `_sl_replay`, `_cut_guard_ts` take `cf = (checks, span, gap)`. #285 item 2: the stepper
judges the loss guards (and the learned cut's guards) from the values `stoploss_params` really uses, stricter borrowed
ones included (`_knob_sl`), not the group's own only.

**break_ok, watch-only (PR5).** The fixed "never at a break" rule becomes the conditional `break_ok(ticker, side, sc,
now, what)` in `stop_loss_leg` and `hedge_guard`: act at a break only on a fresh two-sided book (gap within
`LIVE_SPREAD`) whose pick's mid moved `brk_move` (start 8 points, 4-20) since the break began, held on `BRK_READS` 2
reads `BRK_SPACE` 60 s apart (`brk_fires`), half the move with fresh injury news (`_brk_news`, from a cached report only),
and a fresh order book read (max_age=0) still showing the move. It **never acts in this build**: `BRK_LIVE = False`, and
`brk_on`/`brk_move` are not live knobs (start off per league, borrow `own`). Today it only records break episodes:
`break_watch` (from `auto_sell_now` via `break_watch_held`, and from the guards) notes the pick's bid/mid every 20 s+
from prices the engine already read (`PRICE_SEEN`, no new Kalshi or ESPN request) while the game is at a break, the
price once play resumes, and when the rule would have acted, into table `break_ep`; the leg is also price-watched so its
path with score lines is recorded. The weekly judge (`_knob_res_brk`: selling at the break vs just after it, per
contract) needs `BRK_MIN_N` 40 own episodes and the usual bar; its steps are "would step" only.

**Hedge knobs (PR6), watch-only for now.** The hedge's confirmation counts are split from the loss sale's and are
live knobs (no judge yet, so they stay at their start; their minimums equal the start: 3 checks over 3 minutes, never
looser). `hedge_lock_share` (the 95%) and `hedge_lock_cost` (never under 1x: a hedge never locks in a loss) now read
through `hedge_beats(hg, whole, tickers)` / `hedge_lock_cost(tickers)`, but are **not live knobs**: their judge (new
evidence, `hedge_seen_note`: each time auto cash-out weighs a hedge against a due sale, one row per combo per 10 min,
table `hedge_seen` with the whole offer, what the hedge locks in and costs, the combo's cost, your cap, Prefer hedging,
and whether the game was in play on a fresh score and not at a break; `_knob_res_hedge` counts a hedge at what it locked
in on a 2% worse fill, never where the game wasn't in play/fresh) only sees a hedge's least, never how the game ended,
so the code review found it would always push the share up. They step in shadow (`HEDGE_MIN_N` 30 decisions) until a
judge on real outcomes exists. **The hedge cap raise** (Pete's opt-in, ceiling $150): `knob_cap_step` ($10 a run, 3
standard errors, never above $150, never lowering Pete's value) runs as a test only (`HEDGE_RAISE_LIVE = False`);
`hedge_cap_now()` (used by `auto_sell_now` and `hedge_fresh`) already honours a raise, the ceiling and the brake (your
value while any league's brake holds; a live brake clears a raise), so turning it on later is one switch.

**Smart Buy (PR7), watch-only.** `sb_ev_min` (smallest expected profit to recommend, shared with Build's spreads &
totals list) is judged on Smart Buy's checked bets with results (`sbev`: a bet the bar drops loses its result); a shared
knob may now be stepped in shadow when it has a judge, never live. Nothing Smart Buy buys changes. Its other constants
still wait for a judge (re-simulating each bet).
